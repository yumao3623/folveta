以下内容用于目录表单，提交前按平台限制微调；不得添加不存在的用户数、评价、排名、效果或能力。

**Name**

> Folveta

**Tagline**

> Turn course materials into source-grounded study guides.

**Short description**

> Turn course files into cited study guides and optional quick checks.

**Long description**

> Folveta turns course source files into a structured Study Guide with priorities, key concepts, definitions, visible coverage gaps, and source citations. Students can optionally create a five-question Quick Check to review the material. The workflow starts with the course material, keeps the guide grounded in the supplied sources, and makes it clear what the material does not cover. Folveta supports readable course PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and image materials. It is designed for study and review; it does not claim to replace instructors or guarantee learning outcomes. The current offer is Freemium with 2 guides per month, or Pro at $12/month with 10 guides per month.

**Feature / pricing facts**

- Source flow: `course source files → source citations → optional five-question Quick Check`。
- `Freemium: 2 guides/month`。
- `Pro: $12/month, 10 guides/month`。
- 当前不支持音频、视频、URL 或粘贴文本输入，不暗示存在这些能力。
