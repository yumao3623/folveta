# Folveta Design Direction

Status: active design contract for the public site, account workspace, Study Guide, and Quick Check

## Design read

Folveta turns a student's course files into a useful review plan. Every surface should feel like the same calm, well-edited learning workspace: clear enough to trust with class material, warm enough to invite a first upload, and structured enough to make the next study action obvious.

The primary visual reference is the **Notion** analysis in the local `design-reference` collection. Folveta adapts its restrained hierarchy, paper-like surfaces, modest rounding, hairline rules, readable product storytelling, and quiet control density. It does not copy Notion's brand colors, proprietary type, or illustration style. Folveta keeps its forest green action color, warm paper canvas, and source blue evidence state.

Reference provenance: `/Users/maoyu/.codex/skills/design-reference/references/design-md/notion/DESIGN.md`, from the pinned local snapshot documented in that skill's `references/source.md`. Student illustrations are composed from the MIT-licensed [IRA Design source project](https://github.com/ira-design/ira-illustrations). The selected upstream components are checked into `scripts/assets/vendor/ira/`, and Folveta scenes are generated by a checked-in build script.

## Audience and point of view

The primary audience is US high-school and college students working through real class material: a professor's PDF, a laptop at the library, a calendar before an exam, a source they need to verify, and a short review loop between classes. Show this work directly. Prefer a desk, laptop, notebook, source page, calendar, Study Guide, flashcards, or a quiet study moment over generic productivity metaphors, trophies, mascots, or abstract AI effects.

Student figures are faceless, diverse contextual figures from the selected open asset source. They are not named characters and do not make demographic claims. Alt text describes the learning action rather than appearance.

## Dials

- `DESIGN_VARIANCE`: 5 — hierarchy, rails, rules, and learning artifacts create variation.
- `MOTION_INTENSITY`: 3 — motion confirms entry, focus, progress, and press states.
- `VISUAL_DENSITY`: 5 — comfortable reading space with compact controls and metadata.

## Tokens and roles

The semantic tokens in `app/globals.css` are the source of truth.

| Role | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | `--background` | quiet page floor |
| Ink | `--foreground` | headings and high-confidence labels |
| Body | `--text-secondary` | explanatory and learning copy |
| Forest | `--primary` / `--primary-active` | primary action, selected navigation, eyebrow |
| Soft green | `--primary-soft` | selection and light positive emphasis |
| Paper | `--surface-subtle` | examples and secondary reading areas |
| Source blue | `--source-blue` | evidence and source state only |
| Rule | `--border-soft` | section rhythm and surface separation |

Prefer a 1px rule, flat fill, or whitespace before adding elevation. Controls use 6–10px radii; larger panels stop at 16px. Decorative rounding belongs to illustrations rather than ordinary content cards.

## Typography

Use **Source Sans 3** for headings, body, controls, labels, and the wordmark. It is loaded with `next/font`, self-hosted by the build, and uses the Latin variable font.

- Available weights: 400, 500, 600, and 700.
- Body baseline: 16px. Long learning copy: 17–18px with 1.6–1.7 line height.
- Controls and labels: 600. Page and section headings: 600–700.
- Headings use restrained negative tracking, normally `-0.015em` to `-0.022em`.
- Preserve the font's natural body spacing. Do not reset letter spacing globally.
- Keep public headlines to two or three desktop lines and use readable mobile wrapping.

Source Sans 3 is chosen for clear English screen reading and a familiar educational-product tone. Do not describe any typeface as universally preferred by an audience.

## Icon and illustration policy

`StudyIcon` in `components/ui/study-icon.tsx` is the canonical product-semantic icon entry point and maps business meanings to Lucide glyphs. Simple directional, disclosure, close, and playback controls may import their Lucide glyph directly, but they follow the same sizing, stroke, color, and motion rules.

- Use `currentColor`, 2px absolute stroke width, rounded Lucide geometry, and no internal multicolor fills.
- Metadata icons are 16px; buttons are 18–20px; navigation and status icons are 20px; emphasized frames are at most 24–28px. A standalone functional glyph must not exceed 32px.
- Color belongs to the button, frame, alert, or selected surface. Source state uses source blue; success, warning, and destructive states use their semantic tokens.
- Uploaded sources use file/document semantics. Never use a globe, because Folveta works from user-provided material rather than open-web search.
- Icon buttons have at least a 44×44px hit area and an accessible name.
- Only loading may loop indefinitely. Other icon motion is a short state confirmation and must respect reduced motion.
- The custom open-book brand mark is the sole UI-icon exception. It uses forest ink, paper fills, and one source-blue bookmark. Rebuild PNG and ICO derivatives from `app/icon.svg` with `node scripts/assets/build-brand-icons.mjs`.
- Regenerate stable public icon files with `node scripts/assets/export-study-icons.mjs`.

`AssetIllustration` is reserved for narrative scenes, page support art, and meaningful empty states. A spot illustration may combine one student with two or three learning objects around one visible action. Keep the asset boundary transparent and avoid sticker piles, baked text, facial detail, mascots, emoji, gradients, and decorative shadows.

Every illustration must have its source, license, role, and alt text recorded in `public/illustrations/student-illustrations.json` and `docs/visual-system-v3-assets.md`. Do not add an unlicensed scrape or an improvised generated raster.

## Layout and component language

- Public pages use the shared `SiteHeader` and `SiteFooter` in a 1140px shell.
- Public content favors a strong reading column, a quiet illustration rail, and full-width section rules. Avoid three equal feature cards for instructional content.
- Legal and contact pages use compact heroes so useful content begins in the first viewport.
- The account and learning shells use a 15rem sidebar from 1024px upward. Smaller screens use a compact top navigation.
- Study Guide pages place title, coverage, the Quick Check action, and the first study priority before supporting detail.
- Quick Check is a focused assessment surface. At 390×844, the question and at least one full answer must remain visible above the action toolbar.
- On source-processing screens, place generation readiness and the primary action after the source summary; individual file details follow.
- Primary buttons use forest fill, white text, a 10px radius, and a 48px maximum standard height. Secondary buttons use a quiet surface and 1px rule.
- Step lists use one vertical rule with numbered markers. FAQs use open rules rather than a boxed accordion stack.
- Avoid nested cards. A container must communicate an interaction, state, repeated record, or real grouping.

The first viewport always answers: what is this, who is it for, and what should the student do next?

## Motion

CSS handles the current motion: short entry transitions, button press feedback, loading, and result confirmation. GSAP is reserved for a coordinated scroll narrative, a pinned learning sequence, or another interaction where a timeline materially improves understanding. If GSAP is introduced, scope it to the component, animate transforms and opacity, clean it up on unmount, and provide a reduced-motion path.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop: public heroes use a readable copy column and quiet art rail; learning pages use the compact sidebar.
- Tablet and 1024–1279px laptops: keep the sidebar and one main reading column; do not fall back to a phone-style shell.
- Mobile: one reading column, compact supporting art, 44px control targets, and no content hidden behind fixed navigation.
- Actions wrap without forcing equal widths unless a two-button mobile toolbar needs equal reach.
- Meaning cannot depend on hover. Long labels truncate only where the full value remains available elsewhere.
- Validate at 390px, 768px, 1100px, and a wide desktop viewport in Chrome.

## Quality bar

Before calling a design pass complete, check real copy, title wrapping, focus outlines, contrast, source semantics, reduced motion, keyboard flow, fixed-toolbar overlap, horizontal overflow, and the full upload → Guide → Quick Check path. Run lint, typecheck, unit/workflow tests, build, and browser checks. Visual review must include both desktop and mobile Chrome.
